## 🚀 Launch platforms

Daily or weekly launch boards, the closest thing to Product Hunt.

- [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/) - The original daily launch board. Big audience, strong competition.
- ⭐ [Nick Launches](https://nicklaunches.com/?ref=best-directories) - Weekly launches for makers, with a permanent listing and backlink.
- ⭐ [SaaSCity](https://saascity.io/?ref=best-directories) - Launch your SaaS as a building on an interactive city map.
- [BetaList](https://betalist.com/) - Early-stage startups looking for their first users.
- [Uneed](https://www.uneed.best/) - Daily launches of new tools, with a weekly ranking.
- [Peerlist Launchpad](https://peerlist.io/launchpad) - Weekly launches voted on by a professional network of builders.
- [Microlaunch](https://microlaunch.net/) - Month-long launches, so a product is not decided in a single day.
- [Fazier](https://fazier.com/) - Daily launch platform for makers and indie hackers.
- [DevHunt](https://devhunt.org/) - Launch platform for developer tools.
- [TinyLaunch](https://www.tinylaunch.com/) - Weekly launches for small and indie products.
- [Firsto](https://www.firsto.co/) - Launch platform for new products and startups.
- [Huzzler](https://huzzler.so/) - Launch platform and startup directory for SaaS founders.
- [Twelve Tools](https://www.twelve.tools/) - Twelve featured tools at a time, rotated regularly.
- [OpenHunts](https://www.openhunts.com/) - Weekly launch board for indie makers.
- [Startup Fame](https://www.startupfa.me/) - Launch and showcase platform for startups.
- [LaunchIgniter](https://launchigniter.com/) - Weekly product launches for makers.
- [Tiny Startups](https://tinystartups.com/) - Newsletter and directory for small bootstrapped startups.

## 💬 Communities

Places where a good launch post beats a listing.

- [Show HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/show) - Hacker News' "Show HN" posts. Strong for developer tools; be direct and technical.
- [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/products) - Product pages and a community of bootstrapped founders.
- [r/SideProject](https://www.reddit.com/r/SideProject/) - Share side projects and get honest feedback.
- [r/alphaandbetausers](https://www.reddit.com/r/alphaandbetausers/) - Find early testers.
- [r/startups](https://www.reddit.com/r/startups/) - Read the rules first; self-promotion is limited.
- [DEV Community](https://dev.to/) - Write a launch post about how you built it. Good for developer products.

## ✅ Launch checklist

1. **Prepare assets once.** Logo (square PNG), 3-5 screenshots, a one-line tagline, a 160-character description and a longer one.
2. **Start with the featured platforms and the top launch boards.** A few good launches beat a hundred low-quality listings.
3. **Spread launches over weeks,** not one day, so each one gets attention.
4. **Answer every comment** on launch day. Engagement moves rankings on most boards.
5. **Add evergreen listings** (SaaS directories, AI directories) after the launch, for search traffic and backlinks.
6. **Track referrals** with a `?ref=` parameter or UTM tags so you know which directories send real users.
